How We Help You

We make sure you get good, healthy meals safely and easily in three simple steps.

1. Easy Intake Sign-Up

Fill out a simple form or ask your family caregiver to help you. Our systems will automatically map your home address. Seniors under 10km get a hot meal every day; others receive convenient frozen packages.

2. Healthy Cooking & Temperature Checks

Our professional kitchen partners cook healthy food according to your food allergies. We check the temperature of all foods (>140°F) to ensure they are safe and warm before dispatch.

3. Warm Delivery to Your Door

Friendly volunteer drivers bring the warm meals right to your door. They make sure you are safe, verify your signature, and submit a drop-off check-in to your family caregiver.
