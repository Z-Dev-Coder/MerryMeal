The MerryMeal Delivery Cycle

We maintain a rigorous and safe process linking meal prep to delivery validation.

1

Registration & Geofence

Seniors register. The database auto-computes their coordinates. Seniors under 10km get hot meals daily; others receive frozen packs.

2

Kitchen Prep & Temperature Audits

Partner kitchens dynamically scale recipes based on dietary headcounts and must log safety temperatures before dispatch.

3

Rider Dispatch & Proof

Riders accept optimized sequences, deliver, log vital wellness feedback, and collect signatures or photo proof.
